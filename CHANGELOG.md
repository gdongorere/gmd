# Changelog

## Unreleased — Time machine and the 3D Solar System

- `/stars` boots at the real current time, shows UTC and local clocks (with fullscreen), and can travel to any date.
- Sun position now comes from astronomy-engine in 1800–2200 (Meeus outside); added nutation, equation of time,
  seasons, perihelion/aphelion, Moon phase disc and eclipse flags.
- New 3D Solar System (three.js) with a scale ladder and **Take me home** descent from the Solar System to Earth.
- Orrery: orbit and label toggles, orbital periods, click-to-focus planets, Earth–Moon inset.
- Earth: tilt panel (±10,000 yr), optional schematic exposed shelf for ice ages.
- Galaxy: Sun uncertainty ellipse and spiral-arm drift fan.
- Time UI: keyboard shortcuts, bookmarks, a guided time-travel tour.
- Unverified inputs (galactic constants, Mars storm onsets, ice-age anchors) are labelled as such in the UI and docs.
